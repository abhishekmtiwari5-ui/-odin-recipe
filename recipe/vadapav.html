Vada pav is an iconic, budget-friendly street food from Mumbai, Maharashtra. Often called the Indian vegetarian burger

it consists of a hot, spicy deep-fried potato fritter (vada) tucked inside a soft, fluffy bread roll (pav). It is served with punchy garlic and coriander chutneys and a salted fried green chili

Ingredient

  • pav
  • potatoes
  • gram flour
  • Chillies
  • Spices
  • Chutney

Recipe

  1. Heat 1 tsp oil in a pan, add 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, a pinch of hing, and 7-8 curry leaves.Add 1 tbsp minced ginger, 1 tbsp minced garlic, and 1-2 chopped green chillies; sauté for 30 seconds.
  2. Stir in 1/2 tsp turmeric powder and salt to taste.Add 3-4 boiled, peeled, and coarsely mashed potatoes. Mix well, cook for 2 minutes, turn off heat, and let cool.
  3. Shape the mixture into equal-sized round balls.In a bowl, combine 1 cup gram flour (besan), 1 tbsp rice flour (for crispiness), 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, 1/2 tsp red chilli powder, a pinch of hing, and salt.
  4. Gradually add water to whisk into a smooth, thick, and lump-free batter (similar to a coating consistency). Add a pinch of baking soda right before frying if desired.
  5. Heat oil in a deep kadhai on medium flame.Dip each potato ball into the besan batter to coat it completely.Drop carefully into hot oil and fry until light golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels.
  6. Slit the pav buns in the center without cutting them all the way through.Apply green mint-coriander chutney on one inner side and dry garlic chutney on the other.
