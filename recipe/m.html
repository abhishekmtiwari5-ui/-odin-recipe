A samosa is a popular fried or baked pastry with a savory filling, usually including spiced potatoes, onions, peas, or meat. Originating in Central Asia before the 10th century, it is now a famous snack across South Asia, the Middle East, and worldwide.

Ingredient

  • Green peas
  • potatoes
  • ginger
  • Chillies
  • Spices
  • Chutney
  • Fresh Coriander
  • Oil
  • Asofoetida

Recipe

  1. Dough
    • Combine: Rub flour, salt, ajwain, and ghee together.
    • Knead: Add water gradually to make a stiff dough.
    • Rest: Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Filling
    • Spice: Fry cumin, fennel, coriander seeds, ginger, and chilies.
    • Season: Stir in dry spice powders and salt.
    • Mix: Add boiled potatoes and peas. Cool completely.
  3. Shape
    • Roll: Roll dough portions into thin ovals.
    • Cut: Cut each oval in half into semi-circles.
    • Cone: Wet straight edges and seal into a cone.
    • Stuff: Fill with potatoes, pleat back edge, and pinch shut.
  4. Fry
    • Low: Fry in low-medium heat oil for 10 minutes.
